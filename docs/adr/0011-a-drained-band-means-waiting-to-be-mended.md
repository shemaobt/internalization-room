# A drained band means waiting to be mended, not wrong

## Context

During the **Back-translation** the **Necklace** draws each **Stretch** as a **Band**. When
a **Finding** points at one of them, that band has to say something, and the obvious reading
— "this one is wrong" — is not the one the team needs.

## Decision

A drained **Band** means the **Stretch** is waiting to be mended. It fills again the moment
the team takes the **Mend** on: choosing which voice speaks again, or opening the
microphone. It does not wait for the recording to be delivered.

## Consequences

Choosing, recording and uploading is the whole span in which the team is working, and the
**Necklace** was reporting all of it as nothing done. The accepted cost is that while the
recording runs, mending and mended look the same on the cord; the circle is what says a
capture is in the air. A capture that fails drains the band again, because filling it is a
promise and a promise nobody kept has to be taken back, and a second **Finding** on the same
stretch drains it too — filling is never final.

## Note of 2026-09-25

The band is gone with the cord (ADR 0040); the meaning stays on the Bead row: a drained bead is
waiting to be mended, not wrong.

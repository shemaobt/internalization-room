# A new mother tongue voids the old bridge

## Context

The **Long way** is two stations: the **Stretch**'s **Mother tongue** is recorded again,
and then that same stretch is told again in the **Bridge language**. Between them sits a
window in which the stretch already has new mother-tongue audio while the previous telling
still exists on disk. The field reported the silent bridge player in that window as a
defect.

## Considered Options

Keeping the previous telling audible across the window, so the team could still hear what
it had said before. Rejected because it offers the team a telling that no longer describes
the recording underneath it, which is the one thing the **Analyst** will be asked to judge.

## Decision

Once a **Stretch**'s **Mother tongue** has been recorded again, the previous
**Bridge language** telling is neither audible nor valid. The re-recording mints a **Take**
of its own, and the stretch standing on it carries no telling at all until the second
station supplies one.

## Consequences

The bridge player is silent through the middle of the **Long way**, by rule rather than by
accident, and a reader finding it silent there should read this file rather than file a
bug. A **Stretch** left with a new recording and no telling is one the round's own gate
holds the whole **Passage** for, which is what keeps the window from becoming a resting
state.

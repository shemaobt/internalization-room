# A missing inside a stretch offers both microphones

## Context

A missing **Finding** addressed inside a **Stretch** had been forced down the **Long way**:
one screen, one microphone, the **Mother tongue** recorded again and the stretch told again
over it. The product owner reopened it — a gap in the content need not mean the recording
was wrong; sometimes the team simply left something out of the telling.

## Considered Options

Keeping the forced single microphone, on the reading that a missing is always a gap in what
was recorded. Rejected: it spends the team's recording to fix a telling.

## Decision

A **Missing with an address** offers both microphones, exactly as any other **Finding** that
names a **Stretch** does. The team chooses the **Long way** or the **Short way**. What the
room and the server refuse is the other thing: a newly recorded **Mother tongue** arriving
with the old telling still attached to it. Correcting the recording alone is not a state
this product has — the telling that belonged to the recording being replaced never carries
over, and the second station follows on its own.

## Consequences

The single-microphone screen was removed, which retired ADR 0003. The routing lost its last
dependence on
the kind of **Finding**, so every addressed finding now takes one path. The refusal is a
contract rather than a guard the team can see: nothing on the tablet offers the combination
in the first place, because the station that records the **Mother tongue** cannot end there.

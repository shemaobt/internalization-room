# The recording home takes no recording while the room's listing is read

This amends ADR 0117 without editing its decision; it adds "amended by 0181" to its status
line. Henok decided on 4 November that the rows take no tap until the listing answers (ENG-2162).

## Context

ADR 0117 rebuilds the tablet's parts from the room's listing on a relaunch. The home opens at
once and lays the listing in behind its rows. On a slow room that took 8 to 20 seconds, and for
that time the rows showed no part, «Gravar» was lit, and a tap recorded over a part the room held.

## Decision

- **While the home reads the room's listing, its rows are dimmed and take no tap, and the home
  says «pensando…».** The record controls, the sheet door and the grain controls wait, as they do
  while a grain switch is in the air.
- **The read ends when it answers or fails.** A failed read opens the rows with what the tablet
  holds.

# The dimmed «terminei» is written in ink

While the check cannot be asked for yet, the «terminei» disc keeps its dimmed look and writes
its label in ink at full strength, light and dark, where Marcia's app fades the whole button with
a white label (`.draft-save:disabled`, `app/globals.css:355-357`, at 18fa7c4): this departs from
her app on João's call (ENG-1960). In the light look white does not read on the dimmed disc
whether it fades or not (1.17:1 to 1.64:1), so the label takes ink, #0A0703 at 11.2:1 or better.
In the dark look white read 1.28:1 to 2.17:1 only because it faded with the disc; drawn at full
strength it would read 7.0:1, and ink #F2EDE1 reads 6.57:1 or better. Ink is kept in both looks as
one rule, the text colour, as ADR 0147 does for the areia disc. The lit disc keeps its white
label, and «conferindo…», always on the dimmed disc, is written the same way.

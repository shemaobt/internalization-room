# The number on the areia disc is written in ink

The scene rehearsal's areia disc writes its number in ink, light and dark, where Marcia's app
writes it white (`.bt-n`, `app/globals.css:448-450`, at 18fa7c4): this departs from her app on
João's call (ENG-1956), because white on areia is 1.65:1 and does not read on a tablet in daylight.

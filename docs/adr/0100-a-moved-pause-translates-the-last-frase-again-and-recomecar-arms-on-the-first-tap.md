# A moved pause translates the last frase again, and «recomeçar a tradução» arms on the first tap

On the scene rehearsal's translate step (ENG-1349), a pending pausa shows her adjust row only on a
take with loudness to read; an m4a take keeps its pausa at the tap. Only the last frase's pause
moves: tapping the last bar opens the row at that frase's end, and the circle posts the frase again
over the same start and the new end, naming the frase it replaces. An earlier bar translates its
frase again over its own place, with no row. The room refuses a move in three ways, the Ensaio
Final's own codes (PAUSE_ONLY_ON_THE_LAST_FRASE, PAUSE_PAST_THE_END, PAUSE_CANNOT_STAND_THERE), and
each one drops the move, lets the frase go, shows her line for it and reads the frases again. It is
not «não entendi»: the ticket's lines win over ADR 0101's rule for refusals that name the frase.
A moved pause held to be sent again and refused at «terminei» stops the send, and her line for the refusal stands; a second «terminei» sends without the move. Play during a move drops it, as play drops any pending pausa. «recomeçar a tradução» takes two taps
on the translate step and on places unknown, with the retro's 600 ms wake and 6 s lapse. Its armed
state and her lines (the restart answered or failed, and the three refusals) sit beside the scene
rehearsal's screen, not in it, so their timers never replace a screen a call waits on.

## Consequences

- A team on an m4a take translates the last frase again over its fixed span and must use
  «recomeçar a tradução» to change a pause.
- A pause moved on one tablet can be overlapped by a frase another tablet appends after the move
  landed; the Definer left that residual out of ENG-1349.

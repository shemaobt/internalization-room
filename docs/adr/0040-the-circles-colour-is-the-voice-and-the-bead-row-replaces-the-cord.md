# The circle's colour is the voice and its motion the direction, and the bead row replaces the cord

## Context

The room is wordless (ADR 0014) and has one gesture (ADR 0013), so the shape, colour and
motion of each control are the team's whole vocabulary. By 2026-09-24 that vocabulary had
drifted station by station: six ways to start a capture in three colours, three recording
and five playback animations, four microphone glyphs that only navigate, a cord under the
back-translation and a two-column grid on the findings, and a circle that meant the Guide's
voice in one station and the team's microphone in the next. On the by-hand test a team
answering the Guide tapped the green microphone of the Conversation and landed in the
Rehearsal.

## Considered Options

**Unify the colours only.** Rejected: the glyphs would still lie (a microphone that
navigates).

**The circle only speaks; an azul microphone button records everywhere.** Rejected: it adds
a control to the Invitation and the Conversation and reads ADR 0013 backwards.

**Keep the cord and the reading head.** Rejected: two progress drawings (necklace beads and
a cord with bands) for two stations of the same passage, and the cord's bands were too
thin a target for the team to tap.

**A grid, a single row or an automatic listen for the findings.** Set aside: all three kept
the mend on a screen of its own.

## Decision

**The Circle is the room's one control, 160 px in every station. Its colour is the voice
and its motion the direction:** telha for the Guide, wood for the Mother tongue, azul for the
Bridge language; ring and ripples inward while the room listens, ripples outward while it
sounds. **The Bead row replaces the cord**: one bead per Part in the Rehearsal and per
Stretch in the Back-translation and the findings, translucent while open or pending, solid
once confirmed, drained while a finding points at it, ringed while current; a tap selects
and plays it. **Every row is fixed**: a button that does not apply is dimmed, never hidden.
**The second tap leaves a recording pending and the green check confirms it**: a pending
part or translation is recorded over by tapping the circle again, is heard on the one play
button, and reaches the server only on the check. **The scissors only cuts.** **The Advance
disc is the only way between stations** and a microphone glyph never navigates. **A finding
is a choice of voice**: the team taps the wood or the azul microphone and the mend happens
on the Rehearsal or the Back-translation it already knows.

## Consequences

ADR 0028 (the reading head's two drivers) is superseded: there is no cord. ADR 0011 keeps
its meaning on a bead: drained means waiting to be mended. ADR 0004 keeps its rule (the team
says where the error lives) and loses its shape (the grid). ADR 0037's Record entry wears a
green check. The Conversation's necklace is untouched: its beads are Meaning Map elements,
another thing. The translation reaches the server on the check, so a team leaving before it
loses a pending translation, as it loses a pending part. The prototype is the Design canvas
"Sala · controles de gravar e tocar" (rows Fluxo · Ensaio, Fluxo · Tradução, Fluxo · Achados);
the work is ENG-1112 and its six slices, one owner of each notifier path at a time.

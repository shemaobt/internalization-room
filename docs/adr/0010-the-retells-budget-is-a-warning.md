# The retells budget is a warning, not a cap

## Context

The room counts how many times a **Stretch** has been told again. When that count runs past
its mark the server says the session needs a person. The room had to decide whether that
message closes anything.

## Considered Options

Treating it as a ceiling. The tablet had exactly that: a guard that swallowed the
**Short way** microphone tap in silence once the mark was passed, while the **Long way** had
no such guard. Teams read it as a broken button and detoured down the long way, which left
empty tellings behind them.

## Decision

The budget raises a **Warning**, never a cap. The room refuses the team nothing when the
mark is passed: it asks for someone to come and watch, and goes on taking turns as before.

## Consequences

The guard came out of the **Short way** microphone. A server the room cannot reach still
closes that button, but now it is visibly inert rather than silently dead, which is the
difference between a team that waits and a team that goes the wrong way round.

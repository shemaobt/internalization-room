# The record entry is open for the whole session

## Context

The **Record entry** — the affordance on the **Conversation** that opens the
**Rehearsal** — was drawn only once the room's voice read `done`. It was written that
way when the room still asked its own recording question and unlocked the recording at
the end of a conversation it judged finished.

Marcia removed the question and the unlock with it. Fixed-utterance answer 15: *"o botão
de gravar fica disponível a sessão inteira. Uma equipe que já internalizou abre a
passagem, ouve a abertura e vai gravar."* Answer 8: there is no fixed invitation to the
rehearsal and no password. ENG-777 deleted the server's half on the express condition
that this entry be open for the whole session first — *"that entry must be present and
reachable for the whole session before this deletion lands, or a declining team loses
its only way to record"*. The deletion landed; the entry did not.

On the by-hand test of 22 September the conversation never reported `done` — a bead of
the first scene stayed merely mentioned and the ledger's pointer never moved — so the
entry never appeared. The Guide had already told the team the app would show them where
to record. The team had no way out of the room.

## Considered Options

**Leaving the gate and fixing what makes `done` late.** Rejected as the whole answer: the
completion floor asks every concrete bead `engaged`, which is Marcia's Hard Rule #2, so a
passage whose silence the team only heard mentioned is *correctly* unfinished. A room
whose only door is a door that opens at the end has that failure for every team that
stops short, and stopping short is ordinary.

**Offering the entry through a standing halt as well.** Rejected: opening the Rehearsal
writes the invite over the room's voice, and the voice is where a halt lives. A team that
left while a halt stood would erase from the screen the halt the **Desk** was attending —
the erasure ADR 0035 and ENG-982 were written against, arriving by another door. Carrying
the halt into the Rehearsal and restoring it on the way back was costed and set aside: it
is three times the work for a case the room already answers by stopping.

## Decision

**The record entry is reachable from the first turn of a passage until the session ends,
and a blocking halt is the one thing that hides it.** It is drawn through every voice the
Conversation can hold, and through a **Warning**, which refuses the team nothing (ADR
0032). While a blocking halt stands it is not drawn, and it returns the moment the Desk
lifts it.

Opening the Rehearsal needs no guard of its own: the gesture already silences both
players and discards a capture in flight, like every other gesture that moves the room
(ADR 0024).

## Consequences

A team that stops short of the floor still leaves with a recording, which is the pilot's
whole point — the rehearsal is the first oral draft, and it does not wait for a ledger to
agree that the conversation is over.

The `done` the conversation reports stops being a door and goes back to being what the
doctrine says it is: a fact the ledger states, which never ends the conversation.

A halt keeps its one lifter. Every other door that writes the invite voice over a state
that should stand remains what it was — a defect where it exists, fixed one named arm at
a time, never by a sweep.

# The team says where the error lives, not the kind of finding

## Context

When the **Analyst** points at a **Stretch**, something has to decide whether the error was
born in the recording — so the **Mother tongue** must be recorded again — or only in the
telling. The **Analyst** cannot answer it: it reads the telling-back against the
**Meaning Map** and never hears the mother tongue at all, so it cannot say whether a
difference came from the telling or from the recording underneath it.

## Considered Options

Deciding the path from the kind of **Finding**. That is what the room did, and it hid the
retell outright for three of the eight kinds, sending the team down the **Long way** for
errors that were only ever a slip of the telling.

## Decision

The room asks instead of guessing. A **Stretch** the **Analyst** names goes to a grid of two
columns and two rows: the column is the voice — the team's own tongue in wood, the telling
in blue — and the row is the verb, listening on top and speaking below. Hearing either voice
is free and settles nothing. The wood microphone means the error was born in the recording,
so the **Mother tongue** is recorded again and the stretch is told again over it, in that
order; the blue one means only the telling slipped. The kind of **Finding** now governs one
thing only: the fallback offered when a finding names no stretch at all.

## Consequences

The grid is shown for every **Finding** that names a **Stretch**, whatever its kind, so the
routing no longer has a table anybody can get wrong. The cost is a screen with four targets
in a room that otherwise has one circle — paid for by drawing the **Long way**'s second
station under the wood column, dimmed, so the two-station price is visible before the team
commits.

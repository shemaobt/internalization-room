# A stretch's place is separate from what it plays

## Context

The **Long way** records a **Stretch**'s **Mother tongue** as a **Take** of its own, so
what a mended stretch plays is a whole new file running from nought to its own length,
while what it occupies is still the few seconds it always was. Read as one pair of numbers,
a six-second stretch mended into a twenty-seven-second take drew twenty-seven seconds of
**Necklace** over neighbours nobody had touched, and its **Passage** read as told far past
its own end.

## Considered Options

Deriving the place by matching the mended take back against the list of parts. Measured to
fail on a resumed session: the take is no part of the **Rehearsal**, the match returns
nothing, the counted ground collapses to zero, and every fresh cut inherits the previous
part's take.

## Decision

A **Stretch** carries two independent facts: its **Place** — the part it sits in and the
interval it occupies there, which no **Mend** ever moves — and what it plays. The
**Necklace**, the counted ground and the next cut read the place; playback reads what it
plays. The place is written into the **Resume point**, because it is the one thing about a
telling-back the server cannot hand back.

## Consequences

There is now a second local source of truth that can drift from the server, and it has: on
a cold resume where the **Composed passage** had not reached the tablet, the locally kept
place still named the old mother tongue. That is the accepted cost of the place surviving
the tablet being closed at all, since only this tablet ever knew which part the stretch
stood in.

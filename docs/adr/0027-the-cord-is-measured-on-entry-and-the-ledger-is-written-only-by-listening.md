# The cord is measured on entry, and the listening ledger is written only by listening

## Context

Entering the **Back-translation** threw the **Ruler** away and rebuilt it as a side effect
of deciding which part to put in the air. That decision walks the parts in order and stops
at the first one with no told ground — which, after a part is recorded again, is that very
part (ADR 0020 leaves its ground bare).

The **Cord** cannot place a **Band** of a part beyond the ruler's end, so every band of
every later part vanished until the replaced part had played through to its end. The team
came back to a cord that had forgotten the work it had already done.

Two jobs were fused in one loop: how long every part is, and which part goes in the air.

## Considered Options

**Growing the ruler only as parts end, and teaching the cord to place a band past the
ruler's end.** Rejected: it moves the guess into the drawing. A band placed against a part
of unknown length is drawn where the sound is not, and the ruler's rule — a part nobody
can measure ends the cord rather than lengthening it by a guess — exists because that was
tried.

**Drawing a drained band over the replaced part.** Rejected: ADR 0011 gives a drained band
one meaning, *waiting to be mended*, and this ground is waiting to be told. Absence is the
truth, and ADR 0020 already said so.

**Reporting every measured part as heard, so the finish stops being refused.** Rejected
outright: the gate reads the **Listening ledger** as evidence that the rehearsal was
heard, and measuring a file is not hearing it.

## Decision

**Entering the back-translation measures every part the player can measure and publishes
the whole ruler, before choosing which part goes in the air.**

The catch-up loop keeps its two other jobs and loses the measuring: it still steps over
the parts told whole, still writes those and only those into the listening ledger, and
still breaks at the first part with no told ground. **A part measured is not a part
heard.**

A measurement of nought is the player with nothing to say about a file, never a part of no
length. Every writer of a part's length goes through one place, which is where that is
refused: the measuring on entry, the part measured the instant it is kept, the landing that
measures the parts behind it, and the part that has just played.

## Consequences

The first clip now starts behind the measuring rather than in the same call that asked for
it. The room stands in its thinking state across that wait, which is the honest answer for
a tablet that is busy, and the gestures that wait on it were already written for a wait
that could happen — it happened whenever anything had been told back. What is new is that
it happens every time, so a cut landing inside it is now an ordinary case rather than a
rare one.

Measuring on entry asks the player for every part each time the team enters the
back-translation, where it used to ask only about the parts it stepped over. A part the
player cannot measure still ends the ruler rather than lengthening it, and still does not
stop the team.

# Internalization Room

The oral translation team's tablet app: it walks the team through a Bible passage without showing text, keeps what the team records and carries what the team tells back to the server, which checks it against the Meaning Map and returns findings. All visible progress is the necklace.

## Language

### Stations

**Station**:
One of the stops a session passes through, from the Choice to the Closing; the server names the one the team is in for the Desk, reading it off what the session holds.
_Avoid_: stage (`SalaStage` is the enum, not the term), phase, screen, step

**Panorama Station** (`panorama`):
The station where the book's Panorama is voiced, reached by tapping the Panorama on the wheel; after its line, the team can answer it out loud.
_Avoid_: invitation, convite, welcome

**Conversation** (`conversa`):
The station where the team describes the passage out loud, and what the team touches fills the necklace.
_Avoid_: conversa in prose (`conversa` is the enum value), telling, narration

**Rehearsal** (`ensaio`):
The station where the team records the passage scene by scene in the mother tongue, one Scene row per scene, listens and records again; the advance disc opens the Back-translation only once every scene has its part.
_Avoid_: ensaio in prose (`ensaio` is the enum value), passage recording (the recording itself is the take)

**Back-translation** (`retro`):
The station where the rehearsal recording plays back and the team tells, stretch by stretch and in the bridge language, what each piece says, until "Conferir a tradução" fires the check.
On screen and in the voice its Portuguese is *traduzir / tradução*; *contar* belongs to the Conversation alone, and *recontar* to the External Check.
_Avoid_: retrotradução, retro in prose (`retro` is the code prefix, not the term), contar de volta, contado de volta, reconto, recontar

**Choice** (`escolha`):
The station where the room starts and where the team picks an entry from her menu, the Panorama or a passage.
_Avoid_: escolha in prose (`escolha` is the enum value), menu for the station (the menu is what it shows), selection, wheel (the wheel is the list of entries, not the station)

**Menu**:
What the Choice writes, as Marcia's picker does: «Escolha a passagem», then one block per book with its pills. In code the Choice's Station is the class `Menu`; in prose the word means the written page only.
_Avoid_: picker, ruler, passage list

**Way back**:
The return to the entry the team last left by the way out, offered on the Choice by the chevron and by the left entry's tinted pill. A Panorama whose line was said comes back in silence on the session the room still holds; any other entry is entered again in the language it was left in (ADR 0062, ADR 0092).
_Avoid_: back button, return, chevron (the chevron is one way to take it)

**Closing** (`fim`):
The last station, where the necklace stays as it hung and the session ends.
_Avoid_: fecho, ending, fim in prose (`SalaStage.fim` is the enum, not the term)

**Step**:
The point the team's work has reached inside a Station, such as telling, awaiting the verdict or checked; it belongs to its Station and ends with it (ADR 0046).
_Avoid_: phase, `BtPhase`, status, stage

**Checagem externa** (`checagem_externa`):
The station a team opens from the Back-translation of an approved draft, where outside listeners hear the passage and retell it. One external check per approved version; the version does the work of a gate (ADR 0111).
_Avoid_: external review, listener screen, Phase 4 (the plan's name)

### Who speaks and who listens

**Team**:
The group of oral translators using the tablet: the room guides them, and they are the only people present in the conversation.
_Avoid_: equipe, user

**Guide**:
The room's voice: the persona speaking to the team throughout the session, with fixed lines and spoken lines generated by the server.
_Avoid_: guia, narrator, facilitator (a person, not the voice), `Facilitator` (the inherited prefix in code)

**Fixed line**:
One of Marcia's pre-approved lines, named by its family and its position in her file (`F2`, `D1`, `P3`). The room voices it from the text it was deployed with and answers its address; the tablet asks for that address once per run and keeps the sound, so a line she re-rules is heard on the next load.
_Avoid_: linha fixa, canned line, static line, fail-safe line (one role a fixed line can serve)

**Instant acknowledgement**:
The fixed line played the moment a team turn ends: one of Marcia's three, picked at random by her formula, never the same twice running, and never before an opening (ADR 0056).
_Avoid_: thinking line, filler, wait line

**Process line**:
One of Marcia's fixed lines for a step of the back-translation (start, tell, unheard, approved), asked of the room by position in the Session language (the Room language on the Choice), never rotated; the approval's line is the fourth. On a Joined recording her Ensaio Final lines are asked the same way by position (`N0` the First listen; `N1` and `N2` as the room names them after it).
_Avoid_: fail-safe (a fixed line that answers a failure and rotates), canned line, linha de processo

**Spoken line**:
A line the server composes for this turn, which the room fetches and caches before saying it.
_Avoid_: linha falada, generated line, TTS, fixed line (hers, by name)

**Fail-safe line**:
The line the room falls back to when it could not compose an answer. It is a reply like any other: «Ouvir de novo» says it again (ADR 0152), except a retro verdict the room could not compose, which is not kept and leaves the line before it to be said again.
_Avoid_: linha de segurança, fallback, fixed line (the mechanism rather than this role)

**Analyst**:
The server entity reading only what was told back, comparing it against the Meaning Map and returning findings. It never hears the mother tongue and never speaks to the team.
_Avoid_: analista, classifier, checking AI, checker

**Validator**:
The server entity checking the Guide's draft line before it is said to the team, and able to refuse it.
_Avoid_: validador, correction check (another role), reviewer

**Correction check**:
The server entity checking whether a mend answered the finding, counting what the stretch carried and what the new telling brought back.
_Avoid_: verificador de correção, analyst, validator

**Facilitator**:
The person the room calls when it cannot carry on alone, and who answers through the Desk.
_Avoid_: facilitador, guide, consultant (the `Facilitator` prefix in code names the Guide's voice, not this person)

**Desk**:
The facilitator's web app, where they see the teams, the questions and the halted rooms, mark a halt as answered, and open a session's page to read its stations.
_Avoid_: mesa, panel, dashboard

**Session page**:
The Desk's page for one session, with a tab for the Conversation, one for the passage (the Rehearsal and the Back-translation, part by part) and one for the Approval; it opens on the station the team is in and marks that station on its tab.
_Avoid_: session detail, session view, página da sessão in English prose

**Channel**:
What the room hears or sounds at one moment: silence, an open microphone and whose it is, the Guide speaking, a part or a stretch playing, or a pause. One thing at a time (ADR 0046).
_Avoid_: audio state, voice (the Circle's colour), player

**Microphone owner**:
Whose open microphone the Channel holds: the conversation's, the rehearsal's, the capture's, the question's, the scene rehearsal's, a scene frase's or an outside listener's. The circle's words follow it.
_Avoid_: listening (the circle's voice, not the fact), recording flag

**Microphone answer**:
How the recorder answered the microphone the room asked for: an opening started, refused or failed; a close with the take, or with none; a discard; or a late start abandoned. Every answer but a start, and but a close the recorder failed, gives the Channel back (ADR 0057). On the Scene rehearsal, the Rehearsal and the Back-translation, a refused or failed opening only gives the Channel back: no sign, no line, no count. Only the conversation's microphone on the Conversation or the Panorama counts: a refused opening there shows the person sign at once, a failed one the second time in a row (ADR 0089, 0108).
_Avoid_: capture result (a Capture is the back-translation's microphone), recorder state

**Gesture on its way**:
A gesture of the team from the moment it starts until everything it started has ended, the gesture it hands off to included, or until the room gives up on what that gesture was waiting for. Its own sounds pass, the instant acknowledgement of a turn among them; every other line waits until it ends (ADR 0049).
_Avoid_: busy, pending gesture

**Queued line**:
A line, spontaneous or the answer to a gesture, that arrived while the Channel was not free (a sound playing, the microphone open, a blocking halt standing or a gesture on its way), played once it is free; one of each kind waits, in arrival order, except a gesture's own line, which enters ahead and is never dropped (ADR 0049).
_Avoid_: pending line, interrupted line

**Interruption**:
The team's tap on the circle while the Guide speaks in the Conversation. It stops her and opens the microphone, and the next turn sent tells the room where she was cut: how far into the line (`at`) and how long the line was (`of`, when the tablet knows it). A take too short to keep, or none, tells nothing. The cut line counts as heard, and «Ouvir de novo» says it whole (ADR 0067).
_Avoid_: barge-in, interrupted line (a **Queued line** is not cut)

**Outside listener** (*ouvinte*):
A person who did not help translate and is given the next number of that version, never a name: «ouvinte {n}». Numbers only go up and are never reused; a new version starts again at 1.
_Avoid_: tester, reviewer, participant

### What is recorded and told

**Passage**:
The portion of Scripture the team works on, identified by its pericope, with the reference audio and the Meaning Map elements.
_Avoid_: passagem, pericope (the passage's identifier, not the passage), text

**Reference**:
The passage's Scripture reference as its Meaning Map's heading writes it, with the book named in the Room language — «Rute 1:1–5»; the only words on a passage's pill.
_Avoid_: title, label, name

**Pill**:
One entry written on the menu, tapped once to open it: «◈ Panorama do Livro» for the Panorama, or a passage's Reference.
_Avoid_: button, chip, notch

**Session**:
One team's work on one passage, with state and a persisted snapshot allowing it to resume at the exact station where it stopped.
_Avoid_: sessão, passage, round

**Room language**:
The language the team chose on the Choice, «PT» or «EN», remembered on the tablet and Portuguese when nothing was chosen. The screen and the sessions opened from then on follow it; the device's own language never decides it.
_Avoid_: device language, locale, idioma do aparelho

**Look**:
Light or dark, chosen on the Choice with the sun or the moon and remembered on the tablet. It follows the device until the team chooses.
_Avoid_: theme, appearance, mode, One look (a different thing: the turn read once)

**Session language**:
The language a session was opened in, fixed at the tap that opens it. A session keeps it when the Room language changes.
_Avoid_: current language

**Wheel**:
The list of entries the room offers: the lists of every Served book, in the room's order, written on the Choice as her menu, which the room reopens on its own after the Closing. Not yet read and holding no passage are different states: holding no passage means every book is finished.
_Avoid_: roda, passage list, carousel, choice (the station that shows it)

**Take**:
A recording the team kept, identified locally by its scope (part or whole passage) and, after upload, by the server's identifier.
_Avoid_: gravação, recording (too general), audio

**Recording time** (`recorded_at`):
The moment a take's recording stopped, on the tablet's own clock, read from the recording file when the take enters the Outbox. The Outbox row keeps it with the take's length (WAV only) and fingerprint, and every try sends the three unchanged, however late. The tablet never corrects its clock; the server notes how far off it was, on a joined part only.
_Avoid_: upload time, timestamp, created_at

**Accidental tap**:
A scene take or a part too short to be anything the team said, or one the recorder handed back nothing for. It leaves nothing behind (ADR 0086).
_Avoid_: empty take, short take, capture guard (the Conversation's window)

**Capture guard**:
The short window after the recorder of a Conversation or Panorama take starts in which the team's next tap on the circle is ignored instead of ending the take: the take goes on recording, and nothing is sent or thrown away. A tap while the recorder is still starting is ignored the same way. A tap after the window ends the take and sends it, unless the take is still a ghost, too short or too light, which is thrown away with nothing sent. This also covers the take an Interruption opens.

**Mother tongue**:
The team's language, in which the passage is recorded in the Conversation and the Rehearsal, and which nobody in the room needs to understand.
_Avoid_: língua materna, materna (only as a short form in prose), L1, the team's language

**Bridge language**:
The language in which the team tells back what it heard, so the analyst can check it. In this project, Portuguese.
_Avoid_: língua-ponte, L2, Portuguese (it is the role, not the fixed language), bridge

**Stretch** (`segment` on the wire):
A piece of the back-translation already told: it points at a rehearsal take and at a time range inside that file, never at the concatenated passage.
_Avoid_: trecho, segment (the wire name), chunk (the ephemeral position)

**Pause** (`pausa`):
The point where the team says a frase ends while listening back, snapped to the middle of the nearest silence; moved and heard with her three controls «um pouco antes», «ouvir a pausa» and «um pouco depois». A Stretch ends at its pause, and so does a Scene frase.
_Avoid_: cut, corte, Cortar aqui (ruled out by Marcia on 2026-09-15), split point, boundary

**Restart** («recomeçar a tradução»):
Two taps on the scene rehearsal: the first arms it and names how many frases it discards, and it disarms by itself after six seconds; the second sets those frases aside in the room and keeps the recording. Nothing is deleted.
_Avoid_: reset, Zerar (the facilitator's)

**Capture**:
The back-translation's open microphone, between the scissors and the tap on the circle. A closed capture becomes a stretch; a halt that lands inside one closes it and keeps nothing.
_Avoid_: recording, gravação, chunk (the wire's name)

**Place**:
The part a stretch sits in and the interval it occupies there, which is also what the stretch plays; no mend moves it.
_Avoid_: lugar, position, offset, range

**Untold stretch**:
A stretch recorded in the Rehearsal that was never told back at all, named by the verdict under its own address and never under a finding's.
_Avoid_: trecho não contado, missing (a finding kind), unexplained stretch

**Unheard part**:
A part of the rehearsal the tablet's report does not cover, named by the verdict under its own address and never under a finding's; the room lands the team on it instead of leaving the press dead.
_Avoid_: parte não ouvida, untold stretch (never told; this is never heard), unplayed part

**Untold part**:
A current part of the rehearsal over which no stretch was told back, named by the verdict by its take and never under a finding's; the room lands the team on it from its beginning, the circle ready to tell.
_Avoid_: parte não contada, unheard part (heard by nobody; this one was heard and told nothing), untold stretch (one stretch never told; this is a whole part)

**Cursor**:
The point inside a part's own file where the telling-back stopped, from which a resumed back-translation starts playing and from which it counts what it reports as heard.
_Avoid_: cursor da retro, playhead, reading head (what the necklace draws above the cord)

**Head**:
The position inside the open part or stretch that the scissors, the Capture and the Listening ledger read: the Cursor until the player confirms the opening.
_Avoid_: playhead, reading head (retired), position

**Wordless recording**:
A recording whose transcription holds no words. On the telling doors the server refuses it with no line, and it refuses the same way when its transcriber fails or answers too late. The room stays paused on the same stretch, shows Marcia's line «Não entendi — traduzam de novo esse pedaço» beside the circle and voices nothing, and the circle reopens the microphone for that stretch. It is never counted toward calling a person.
_Avoid_: empty capture, silence, inaudible take

**Listening ledger** (`escuta das partes`):
What the team heard of each part of its rehearsal, kept one part at a time, in that part's own milliseconds and keyed by the recording the part was kept as. It holds only what sounded, on the back-translation and on the rehearsal alike, but for a part stepped over because it was told back whole and a part picked up at its cursor over ground already told, both counted from their start, and entering the back-translation no longer forgets it; leaving the passage does. A part recorded again starts its listening over while the others keep theirs. The tablet reports it to the room as each part ends, when the team leaves the back-translation or the passage, when the app goes to the background, and with the check; a part the room has not named yet is left out of every report. Which parts are unheard is always the room's answer, never counted on the tablet (ADR 0105).
_Avoid_: `_ouvido`, registro de escuta, played ranges, the concatenated passage, the rehearsal's whole length

**First listen** (*Primeiro, só ouvir*):
The Back-translation's listen-only step: her line, then every part from its beginning, one after another, with nothing to do but listen. On a Joined recording the line is N0; on a recording no join touched it is P0, and the whole recording always plays. The circle and «terminei» are closed and the bars and frases cannot be tapped; one control pauses and resumes. It runs whenever the room counts every part unheard; on a Joined recording with some part already heard, her P2 and only the unheard parts play the same way. Translating opens only once the room counts every part heard, and the room's answer to that last report says what follows; on a recording no join touched that is always P1 and the recording again from its first part (ADR 0123).
_Avoid_: escuta inicial, preview, playthrough, «Ouvir tudo» (the Rehearsal's own play)

**Ruler**:
Where each rehearsal part ends along the cord, derived from the measured length of each part's file. The parts are measured on entry to the back-translation, before the first of them plays. It is drawn only as far as the parts already measured, so a part nobody can measure ends the cord rather than lengthens it by a guess.
_Avoid_: régua, timeline, listening ledger (what was heard, not where a part ends)

**Chunk**:
The numbered position of a stretch in the list the analyst receives in one reading. It exists only during that reading; the server turns the number back into a segment.
_Avoid_: trecho, stretch, segmento, segment (the persistent objects)

**Part**:
One of the pieces the rehearsal recording is divided into, and the unit a stretch sits in. A stretch addresses its part by where the part sits in the row, so a part that moved would take the whole rehearsal with it. A part recorded again keeps its number and its place; what changes is the recording it holds, and the stretches told over the one it replaces are untold ground again. A part recorded again goes up under its own next pass, and a part recorded for the first time under its first. Part N is Scene N; a part keeps the number the room holds for it and is never renumbered by its place in a listing.
_Avoid_: parte, chunk (a position in one reading), take (what holds a part), stretch, composed passage (retired: the take the server assembled around a mend)

**Necklace**:
The cord of beads that is the room's progress indicator for the conversation: its twelve beads, of which as many are lit as the room says, show the conversation's coverage. Drawn over the Conversation and the Closing only; the Back-translation and the findings show the Part strips instead, and the Rehearsal its Scene rows.
_Avoid_: colar, progress bar, ghost bead

**Bead** and **element**:
One of the necklace's twelve, whatever the passage's number of elements. The room says how many are lit (`beads_filled`): the elements engaged over all the elements, times twelve, rounded half up. An **element** is one of the Meaning Map's, and moves through not encountered, surfaced (the Guide said it) and engaged (the team said it); it is not a bead.
_Avoid_: conta, pearl, item, bead for an element of the Map, part bead or stretch bead (retired with the Bead row)

**Part strips**:
The rows at the top of the Back-translation and the findings, one per Part, hers: «parte N» written (or «A passagem inteira» for a whole-passage take) beside the strip of that part's take, its loudness drawn so a silence reads as a valley, each told Stretch a band with a tick at its cut, a stretch of a part recorded again faint and dotted, the pending pausa in telha and the player's line. The strip cannot be tapped (ADR 0153).
_Avoid_: Bead row (retired on 2026-10-27), cord, reading head (retired on 2026-09-25), contas, bits, progress bar

**Circle**:
The room's control on every station but the recording home, whose Scene rows record; its size is hers per screen (ADR 0040). Its colour is the voice (telha the Guide, wood the Mother tongue, azul the Bridge language) and its motion the direction (ring and ripples in while the room listens, ripples out while it sounds); the first tap opens the microphone and the second closes it. On the First listen its tap does nothing.
_Avoid_: círculo, facilitator circle, record circle, button, esfera

**Advance disc**:
The wood disc with no glyph that leads from one station to the next: to the Back-translation from the Rehearsal, to the verdict from the Back-translation; it lights only when nothing is pending and pulses while the room beckons; on the Rehearsal it lights only once every scene has its part and every part is on the tablet.
_Avoid_: next button, continue, seguir, microphone (it never wears one)

**Knot**:
A question the team raised at its idle turn, recorded by a tap on the hand and sent to the facilitator. A sent Knot leaves no sign on the tablet, and one whose file is under 800 bytes is dropped unsent, however long it ran: a Knot is held to no time floor (ADR 0067).
_Avoid_: nó, question, bead (a knot is not one)

**Raised hand**:
The team's side channel to the facilitator: it sends a Knot, checks for the facilitator's replies, plays a reply and marks it heard. A failure anywhere on it changes nothing in the room and speaks no line (ADR 0071).
_Avoid_: mão levantada, question button, note

**Set-aside reply**:
A facilitator's reply the Raised hand stops offering after two failures, a sound that did not play or a heard mark the Desk refused; a tap that cuts it is not one. It lives in the tablet's memory only: a new address from the Desk or a relaunch offers the reply again, and the Desk keeps it unheard.
_Avoid_: blocked reply, skipped reply, dropped reply, resposta descartada

**Idle turn**:
The moment the Conversation or the Panorama waits on the team and nothing else is happening: the voice at the invite or done, no line of the room's own playing (a reply may), no Halt calling for a person, no microphone open, and a session to send to. The Raised hand is offered only then, and a Knot armed in it is abandoned when it ends.
_Avoid_: idle, the team's turn (that also covers the open microphone)

**Panorama**:
A book's own entry and the line about the whole book, first in its book's block, «◈ Panorama do Livro», played when the team taps it. Each Served book has its own, opened by its own id (`OV-<Book>`). Not a passage: nothing that counts passages counts it.
_Avoid_: introduction, overview

**Served book**:
A book the room serves; the Choice shows one block per served book, in the room's order.
_Avoid_: the room's book, the book (as if there were one)

**Opening line**:
The opening line specific to the chosen passage, said when its Conversation opens for the first time.
_Avoid_: scene (until 2026-10-13 this entry's name; a scene is now one of the passage's), cena

**Scene** (`cena N` on screen):
One of the passage's scenes as her Meaning Map divides it, numbered by its place in the passage; the unit the team rehearses on the red microphone. On the team's screen it reads «cena N» with its title, her words.
_Avoid_: part (a piece of the rehearsal recording), opening line (the former meaning), section, act

**Scene rehearsal** (`ensaio da cena`):
One recording of a scene in the mother tongue made on the red microphone, translated frase by frase and sent to the voice with «terminei»; numbered per session, never replaced, frozen once sent and always listenable.
Its screen is a Station of its own (`cena`), reached by the red microphone and left by «Voltar para a passagem atual».
The screen lists this session's scene rehearsals: an unsent one resumes where it stood, and a sent one opens to be listened to.
_Avoid_: rehearsal (the station, and the whole passage's recording), take (what holds it), practice, ensaio in prose

**Scene question**:
«Qual cena vocês vão ensaiar?», asked before recording a Scene rehearsal on a passage of two or more scenes, with one button per scene; it may highlight a scene but never picks one.
_Avoid_: scene picker, scene menu

**Scene label**:
The scene the team tapped on the Scene question, read at the tap that starts the recording, or later on the plan's card for an Unlabelled rehearsal (said later); never guessed, and never changed by a retry of the upload.
_Avoid_: scene guess (the classifier's, Observation Room only), sceneId

**Scene frase** (`frase N` on screen):
One frase of a Scene rehearsal, told in the bridge language: the stretch of the take from the previous pause to this one. It is saved by the room at the stop tap with its place in the take, and numbered on screen by its place in the take. A frase translated again replaces it on screen and for the voice; the replaced one stays with the room as history.
_Avoid_: piece, trecho, segment (the back-translation's)

**Translating a frase again** (`traduzindo de novo a frase N`):
The team taps a Scene frase's bar on the translate step: its stretch of the take plays, and the next circle records a new translation of that same stretch, which replaces it. «manter a tradução de antes» gives it up.
_Avoid_: retell (her code's word), re-record (the retro's «regravar a frase», ENG-1221)

**Scene strip**:
The scene rehearsal's wordless picture of its take while translating. The loudness of the take is drawn so a silence reads as a valley. Each Scene frase is a band with a tick at its end, the player is a thin line, and the pending pause is a bold line. It carries no words and cannot be tapped.
_Avoid_: waveform, timeline, ruler (the Ensaio's)

**Scene row**:
One row of the Rehearsal per Scene of the passage, in passage order, reading «Cena N» and the scene's title, holding that scene's Part with its record, listen and record-again controls. Part N is Scene N: a recording made from row N is kept as part N, and a part the room holds under number N shows on row N. A row with a take shows «✓»; a row offers its Rehearsal sheet with «trocar o ensaio desta cena» when its part is joined and its scene has another rehearsal, or «usar o ensaio desta cena» when none of its takes came from a join and a Usable rehearsal exists.
_Avoid_: bead (the Back-translation's), clip row, part row, linha

**Joined recording** (`origin`):
A rehearsal with at least one current Part laid in from a Scene rehearsal, which the part's `origin` names (the server's term, same field and meaning). On the tablet it turns the Rehearsal's title into «Ensaio Final», and the Back-translation's into «Ensaio Final»; entering it starts the First listen while the room counts every part unheard. The tablet makes one with «juntar as cenas e ouvir» on the Ensaio Final plan. Each scene's chosen rehearsal goes up through the Outbox as that scene's Part: the rehearsal's own bytes under its fingerprint, with `origin`. The same bytes joined again, from any tablet, are the same take (ADR 0120).
_Avoid_: assembled recording, merged, Junção (in prose)

**Rehearsal swap**:
«usar este» on a Scene row's Rehearsal sheet: that Scene rehearsal goes up through the Outbox as the Part's next pass, carrying `origin`, and becomes the part once the room names it. The take it replaces is kept on the tablet and in the room, the part starts unheard, and the stretches told over the old take leave the check as history; swapping back to an earlier rehearsal is a new take again. Over an approved draft, or over a take already standing under the scene, it takes the Armed tap.
_Avoid_: Swap (the room's word for a finding of two exchanged relations), troca, replace

**Bring-back** («trazer de volta as traduções do ensaio desta cena»):
The armed tap on a joined Scene row that lays its Scene rehearsal's frases over the Part again, when none of them stands there: after the translation was restarted, or when they never came. The stretches told here for that scene are kept as history, never deleted. The room says which parts can take it: never a part a sentence was re-recorded in, nor one joined from the other language's session.
_Avoid_: reimport, restore, undo

**Red microphone**:
The round red control beside the orange dot that opens the Scene rehearsal's screen, shown only while the Conversation rests and no raised-hand note or person call stands. With the orange dot it is one of the two ways from the Conversation to a recording.
_Avoid_: orange dot (the other way, into the Rehearsal)

**Orange dot**:
The small orange control at the Conversation's top right that opens the Ensaio Final: the Ensaio Final plan as its entry reads (today, over a passage with no take) and the recording home when there is a take; the join case arrives with ENG-1385. Faint and open whenever the Conversation rests, also before the send-off; bright from the voice's send-off, the completion floor or the Ensaio Final moment on; gone while any voice sounds or thinks, a microphone other than the raised-hand note's is open, a sent note is on its way, or a person is called; never on a Panorama. Its name is «Gravar o ensaio de vocês», and «Abrir o Ensaio Final» once a part came from a join (ADR 0117).
_Avoid_: record entry, record button, botão de gravar, green check, green microphone (the glyphs the way into the Rehearsal wore until 2026-10-21), handoff, rehearsal gate (it gates nothing)

**Opened**:
A session that holds a Guide line, its Opening at least. The room asks a session's Opening only while it is not opened.
_Avoid_: started, begun

**Store build**:
The app as the stores hold it: the build installed on every tablet from the App Store or Google Play, which reads the room's **Minimum build** at launch and on every return to the **Choice** (ADR 0124), and which changes only by a new version published to both stores (ADR 0082, ADR 0110, ADR 0124). A version bump merged to main is the store build's only source.
_Avoid_: release (the server's record of an approved draft), the build (ambiguous), app version

**Minimum build** (`minimum_app`):
The oldest **Store build** the room accepts, per platform: the iOS build number and the Android version code, the `+N` of `pubspec.yaml` (one number on both platforms, ADR 0110), served on `/api/version` as `minimum_app: {ios, android}`. 0 means no minimum. It is raised by hand, in the production deploy file, at a release. A tablet whose own build number is below it shows the **Update screen**. A build that carries no number (0, a laptop build) is never below it.
_Avoid_: minimum version (the `x.y.z` is not compared), app version, forced update

**Update screen**:
The one screen a **Store build** below the **Minimum build** shows, in place of everything else: her-style line «Há uma versão nova do aplicativo. Toquem para atualizar.» and one button that opens the app's own listing in its own store. It stands until the store installs a newer build. Nothing else reaches the team while it shows. It shows only once the room has recorded an arrival at the **Choice** (never for the station it stands on before the launch settles), or once the link read has found the tablet unlinked, and then it stands for the run: a read that finds the build below the minimum while a passage is open is held until the next arrival at the Choice, and the room never waits for that read to open.
_Avoid_: force update, upgrade wall

**Outbox**:
The queue of takes and scene takes waiting to reach the server, with a manifest that survives the app closing and names each recording by file rather than by path. It delivers one part's recordings in the order the team made them; a recording leaves it once the room's own list holds it, never on the upload's answer alone. Each row keeps its recording's **Recording time**, its length when it is a WAV and its fingerprint (sha256), fixed when the row is written; a row an older app wrote has none and sends none; a row of a join also keeps the rehearsal it came from (`origin`), the part's take it replaces, and whether the team armed the reopening of an approved draft, and its Recording time is the moment of each try, which the room reads only as the clock offset. An unsent take is shown on its Scene row, never spoken.
_Avoid_: fila, caixa de saída, upload queue, buffer

**Take in the air**:
The one take the Outbox is sending now, from its first attempt to the moment it is confirmed, spent or refused, with the session it belongs to and when its tap or keep began sending. «parar» is offered 8 s after that and stops whichever take of that tap or keep is in the air; the screen of another session never stops it.
_Avoid_: leaving take, upload in progress, current upload

**Unsent take**:
A take of a Part of this session still on this tablet, the newest pass of its Part, that is still in the Outbox (sending, failed or refused for good), or that this visit kept and could not put in the Outbox. Its Scene row tells it in her words: «enviando…» while it is being kept, with no tap whatever the row read before, or sent, with «parar» once its send has been in the air for 8 s; «falhou — toque para tentar de novo» once its three attempts are spent, the team stopped it, it waits behind a spent take of its Part, or it never reached the Outbox; «falhou — grave de novo», with no tap and nothing to play but the room's own part, once its recording is gone, even after a relaunch, since it can never go up (recording the Part again recovers it); «grande demais para o servidor — grave em partes menores» or «não foi aceita — grave de novo», with no tap, once the room refused it for good; and a take left by an earlier visit with her notice «Uma gravação ainda está neste computador e não foi enviada.» and «Cena N · m:ss — enviar agora». Once the room's list holds it, the row reads «✓ salvo» for the rest of the visit, until the part is recorded again. A tap gives the part three attempts; nothing sends a take but a tap, and a take of a session the room no longer knows is offered on the next visit to its passage and goes into that passage's session. A Part beyond the passage's last Scene has a numbered row of its own while it has a take to tell; that row tells the send and records nothing. A visit is this app run of the session: a relaunch makes every take still waiting one of an earlier visit.
_Avoid_: stranded take, stuck recording, gravação presa, pending take (the Outbox's row)

**Resume point**:
The durable slice of the room's state for one passage, written whole at every transition — the session, the Station and its Step, the kept takes, the Cursor, the pending translation, the Listening ledger and the outside listener in progress (its version and number; what that listener has done is the room's to say, ADR 0116) — so a reopening lands where the team stopped, on a tablet that still holds the recordings or on one that fetches them from the room again. The server rules over what it knows, the slice over the rest (ADR 0046). One per passage and **Session language** (ADR 0077). On the Rehearsal, the parts it names that are not here come down behind their rows (ADR 0128). It never expires by age: what drops it are facts about the room or the passage — the room no longer knowing the session — never the clock. An approval keeps it, on the Back-translation (ADR 0112).
_Avoid_: ponto de retomada, em curso, checkpoint, snapshot (the session's own lives on the server)

**Current session**:
The session this tablet was in when it last stood in a passage, kept on the tablet so a relaunch lands on it while the room still holds it. Let go when the team goes to the Choice, when the passage closes, when the room no longer knows the session and when the Desk unlinks the tablet.
_Avoid_: pointer, last session, sessão atual

**Station's session**:
The session the team is in where it stands: the Panorama's at the Panorama Station, the passage's anywhere else, none on the Choice. Any other session the room still holds is an earlier one (ADR 0092).
_Avoid_: current session (that is the relaunch record), active session

**Listener's retelling**:
The outside listener's own telling of the passage after hearing it whole, recorded on the Checagem externa and stored the moment it stops; never transcribed, never sent to a model.
_Avoid_: feedback, comment (a comment is on the whole or on a frase)

**Armed tap**:
The two-tap gesture that guards a change over an approved draft: the first tap arms with «toquem de novo: isto abre de novo o rascunho aprovado», the second counts only from 0.6 to 6 seconds after it, then the control disarms by itself. The Restart's arming, lifted to one gesture (ADR 0013, 0100).
_Avoid_: double tap, confirm, long press

**Saved screen**:
The screen after an approval: a check mark, «Ensaio salvo», the approved sentence with its version, and «Pronto», which lands the team on the Back-translation of the approved draft.
_Avoid_: success screen, confirmation dialog

**Grain**:
How a passage is recorded: the whole passage in one take, or scene by scene. A switch sets the earlier work aside, never deletes it (ADR 0102).
_Avoid_: mode, recording form (the form is one take's shape)

**Ensaio Final plan**:
The screen the orange dot opens on a passage with scenes while a scene is still left to join and every part so far came from a join: one row per scene with its Default rehearsal and its facts, and «juntar as cenas e ouvir». Once every scene is joined the dot opens the recording home. Facts only, never a word anyone said.
_Avoid_: join screen, summary

**Default rehearsal**:
The rehearsal a scene's plan row holds before the team chooses another: the last one the voice received that is joinable; an unsent one is never the default, and one with an untranslated frase stays the default, marked «tradução incompleta».
_Avoid_: selected rehearsal, latest take

**Rehearsal sheet**:
What a plan row's «trocar o ensaio desta cena» opens: one scene's rehearsals of this session, newest first, each with its facts and «enviado» / «não enviado», «✓ em uso» on the one the row holds and «usar este» on the other Usable ones, sent or not, and «gravar esta cena de novo» at its foot. «usar este» is the only way the team puts a rehearsal other than the Default rehearsal on the plan.
_Avoid_: swap sheet (her code's name), picker, modal

**Unlabelled rehearsal**:
A Scene rehearsal of this session with a translated frase and no Scene label of this passage: recorded while the scene list was missing, or before labels existed. The Ensaio Final plan puts it to the team on the card «De que cena é este ensaio?», one at a time, oldest first, the team stepping through them with previous and next; it is never guessed and never joined until the team labels it.
_Avoid_: unassigned (her code's word), orphan

**Joinable**:
A Scene rehearsal that can be the Default rehearsal: sent, confirmed by the room, and a 16 kHz mono WAV.
_Avoid_: valid, ready

**Usable**:
A Scene rehearsal the team can put on a plan row and join as a Part: confirmed by the room and a 16 kHz mono WAV, sent or not yet sent. Every Joinable rehearsal is Usable; an unsent one is Usable and never the default.
_Avoid_: swappable, eligible

### Findings and mends

**Verdict**:
The check's answer when the team says it has finished telling back: the passage is clean, or it carries findings, or it names an untold stretch to go straight to, or it refuses the check outright and names the parts the team's report does not cover.
_Avoid_: veredito, result, finding (one item a verdict may carry), correction check (the server entity)

**Blocker**:
One of the codes a refused approval names, one string each: the gate's ten plus `no_project`. A blocker says which hole stands; the tablet opens the door it has for it, in its own order, and calls a person for the rest.
_Avoid_: reason, error code, bloqueio, refusal (the answer that carries them)

**Approval**:
The team's gesture, after a clean verdict, that makes the passage its final draft: one press on the back-translation screen, the server's release answered with a version, and Marcia's approved process line, asked of the room by name, then the **Saved screen** over the Back-translation; the passage is not closed (ADR 0112). A refused one shows one of her three texts and takes the team to the hole its blockers name.
_Avoid_: aprovação, finalize, release (the server's record of it), approve button

**Draft** (*rascunho*):
The team's recording of a passage with its frases and telling-back, kept across openings because it is the room's live **Session** of that passage (ADR 0071 of the API). An approval leaves it open under its version; a new take un-approves it.
_Avoid_: rascunho final (the approved version's words on the screen), session (what holds it)

**Approved draft** (*rascunho final aprovado*):
A **Draft** whose **Session read** says `approved`: a re-entry lands on its **Back-translation**, checked, with «✓ aprovado como rascunho final (v{n})» where the approve button stands, its frases and their parts — never on the **Conversation** and never under the **Warning mark**. The badge goes as soon as a session read says the draft is no longer approved or the team leaves the checked phase. Over a forced version the approve button stands, with the badge under it, once the telling is checked, and a press mints the next version (ADR 0150).
_Avoid_: approved mark (the «●» on the Choice), finished passage, closed passage

**Approved mark**:
«●» after the passage's reference, read «tem um ensaio salvo», for a passage the server holds a live release of, from any tablet, forced or not. Read from the room on every load of the Wheel; never from what this tablet started or finished. A Zerar takes it away.
_Avoid_: finished mark, done mark, started mark, ✓

**Finding**:
The analyst's result on what was told back, in Marcia's words: missing, addition or unclear.
_Avoid_: achado, error, problem, the retired kinds meaning change, wrong relation, reordered event, preservation violation and insufficient evidence

**Missing with an address**:
A missing finding whose place (before, inside or after) fits a stretch already told. It goes to the "where the error lives" screen.
_Avoid_: falta com endereço, internal missing, missing with a chunk

**Where the error lives** (*Onde mora o erro* on screen):
The choice the team makes on a finding between the two voices, by tapping the wood microphone (record the Part again, on the Rehearsal) or the azul microphone (translate the Stretch again, on the Back-translation); the mend itself happens on those stations.
_Avoid_: error grid, finding screen, grid (the two-column shape retired on 2026-09-25), Onde mora o erro in English prose

**Missing without an address**:
A missing finding pointing past the last stretch told. It does not become a correction screen: the team goes back to the Rehearsal to record the rest, keeping takes, stretches and necklace.
_Avoid_: falta sem endereço, external missing, missing at the end, missing without a chunk

**Mend**:
The correction of a stretch pointed at by a finding, through one of the two ways below.
_Avoid_: conserto, correction (reserved for the server's check), repair, fix

**Long way**:
The mend that records the stretch's whole part again in place and then tells that part's stretches again in the bridge language. It is the only exit the fallback offers for an addition; a finding that names a stretch offers both ways instead, and the team chooses.
_Avoid_: the long way round, caminho longo, re-recording the stretch (retired: the microphone on one stretch of the mother tongue), mother tongue plus bridge

**Short way**:
The mend that only tells the stretch again in the bridge language, over the mother tongue recording already there.
_Avoid_: the short path, caminho curto, retell only, simple correction

**Borrowed translation**:
The telling a stretch already has on the server, left as the pending translation when a finding sends the team to translate that stretch again. It can be heard and recorded over, is never deleted by this tablet, and the check waits for a telling recorded in this visit.
_Avoid_: old file, lent file, emprestada in English prose

**Resumed back-translation**:
A back-translation carrying on from where it stopped in a reopened session: playback restarts at the cursor and the stretches already told come back from the server.
_Avoid_: retro retomada, resuming from scratch

### The room and the people

**Halt**:
The state in which the room cannot carry on alone and calls for a person, insisting at intervals until it is answered. A halt either blocks, and then only the team's tap on the person sign clears it, or is a warning. A halt never changes the Station or its Step; a blocking one silences the Channel. It is raised only in Marcia's six cases: a spoken turn refused or answered without a reply, a turn given up whose One look finds nothing, a scene rehearsal given up whose look finds nothing or answered without a reply (shown once the team is back on the Conversation), one reply's sound failing to load twice (ADR 0152), a tablet that cannot record (a refused start on the Conversation or the Panorama), and the microphone failing to start twice in a row. Outside them, a halt that calls nobody is raised only by the build guard on a broken build (no room address), or by an `Error` in anything the room hands off; the team's tap clears it without reading anything (ADR 0125). A Session read never raises one (ADR 0093). A halt raised on the Scene rehearsal, the Checagem externa or the Back-translation is not drawn there and cuts nothing: no sound is silenced and no capture closes. It waits for the Conversation and rises there as the halt it was, calling a person only if it did. It is dropped if the team arrives anywhere else first, and on the Back-translation once a line the room serves is heard there to its end (ADR 0159, 0160).
_Avoid_: sala parada, stuck room, pause

**Person sign**:
How a blocking Halt is drawn: a soft wash over the whole screen with the Shema mark and two outlined figures, with no words and no sound. The circle, the moment label, the orange dot, the red microphone and the passage button fade under it. It says no word to VoiceOver and is no button, and while it rises or stands VoiceOver reaches nothing beneath it. While it rises or stands it takes every tap, and a tap anywhere on it clears it and the room rests; as it fades out it takes none, and a tap reaches what lies beneath. Nothing is replayed or resent, and nothing the Desk does clears it or holds it (ADR 0107, 0144, 0159).
_Avoid_: handoff (her CSS name), halt screen, sinal de pessoa, the userCheck glyph (retired)

**Warning**:
The kind of halt that asks for someone to come and watch while refusing the team nothing. It is a warning, not a cap: the room takes turns as before, and it is what the retells budget raises — on every route a telling can take, whether the tablet hears it on a state read or in the answer to a stretch told again. The room raises it by marking the session itself, so the tablet never calls for a person over one, and it ends when the Desk attends the session, in whatever station the team is in.
_Avoid_: aviso, cap, block, halt (the state, of which this is one kind)

**Claquete**:
The small line of text on the session screen giving the passage, the date and time, and the session, so field video can be matched to the session. It is shown briefly, never as a control.
_Avoid_: slate, clapperboard, session label

**Shema mark**:
The mark at the top centre of the Conversation, drawn from the four shapes of Marcia's logo. A tap on it shows the claquete again; it is not read aloud. It is also atop the Choice, where it does nothing.
_Avoid_: logo, watermark, icon

**Warning mark**:
Retired 2026-10-27 (ENG-1831): a standing warning shows nothing on the team's screen, as
Marcia's does; the Desk lists it. The Circle's own colour stays the voice, in every state.
_Avoid_: aviso, warning badge, the green disc (retired 2026-09-25)

**Call for a person**:
The room's action of telling the Desk it needs someone, insisted on at intervals until the call lands or the team's tap clears the sign. It never holds the team: the Desk's visit is a record and changes nothing on the tablet.
_Avoid_: pedir uma pessoa, calling a human, SOS, `needsPerson` (the internal name)

**Watch**:
The room's periodic Session read, beating for as long as a session is open, with or without a halt (ADR 0048). A Session read never raises the person sign nor clears it (ADR 0093, 0107). A halt never stands without it (ADR 0046). A halt that calls nobody, raised by the build guard or by an `Error` in a hand-off, is cleared by the team's tap alone and reads nothing; with no session it has nothing to read either (ADR 0125). It ends with the session.
_Avoid_: poll, timer, vigia (the code's name is not the term)

**Effect**:
What the machine asks the room to do after an event: a sound to play, a timer to arm, a call to make. The machine returns effects as data and never runs them (ADR 0053).
_Avoid_: side effect, command, action

**Effect runner**:
The one place that executes every Effect, through the four Ports, reading the StationHost; the notifier hands it the effects and holds no switch over them (ADR 0053).
_Avoid_: dispatcher, interpreter, handler

**StationHost**:
The one interface the EffectRunner reads from the room: the Station's questions (what is wanted or in course), its hearings (what the runner heard back) and the lifecycle hand-off. It executes no effect.
_Avoid_: EffectHost, callback, delegate

**Port**:
One of the four doors the Effect runner reaches the world through: Room (the server and the person-call inbox), Sound, Recorder and Store (the disk and the Outbox). Each is an interface in the domain with one production adapter (ADR 0053).
_Avoid_: repository (what an adapter wraps), service, gateway

**Sound port**:
The Port for everything that sounds, the Guide's lines and the parts together, so that only one sound plays at a time.
_Avoid_: player, voice (what its two halves are called in the code)

**Generation**:
The machine's count of how many times its in-flight work was abandoned; an answering event from an older generation is ignored (ADR 0053).
_Avoid_: epoch

**Way in**:
A Station with no session yet: the Choice, a passage or a Panorama not opened. A way in whose request fails lets its Step go and rests at the invite, and the team's next tap asks again (ADR 0108).
_Avoid_: reach, offline, out of reach

**Failure policy**:
The one place every room result (answered, network failed, refused with a code, session gone, timed out) becomes an event: the One look, a blocking Halt that calls for a person, the Session gone, the Station's own answer, or the Raised hand's failure, which the machine answers with no change (ADR 0071), and every failure of the tablet's own that may call a person (the reply's sound, the microphone's start, a tablet that cannot record), and the Disk failure at the scene door, which never does; a refusal is never counted, and nothing outside the six cases becomes a Halt, except a broken build (no room address) or an `Error` in a hand-off, whose halt calls nobody (ADR 0093, 0125). A network failure that is no turn's lets its Step go and changes nothing else (ADR 0108). Nothing decides a failure inside a Station or the notifier (ADR 0053), except the plan card's «não deu para guardar a resposta», an inline refusal on the card (ADR 0119).
_Avoid_: error handler, retry policy

**Disk failure**:
The tablet could not keep on its own disk audio the room handed over at the scene door (a scene rehearsal's take or one of its frases). It is a failure of the tablet's own, decided by the Failure policy, never read as a network failure. It calls no person, and the scene screen carries its word on the Step it stands on until the team's next gesture (ADR 0129).
_Avoid_: no network, offline, dead disk (in prose)

**One look**:
What the tablet does once it gives up on a turn, because the network failed, its 305 s wait ran out or the watchdog fired: it reads that one turn on the server without sending it again. Once the room answers that it is still answering the turn, it reads it again every two seconds, under the busy watchdog, and only a reply that landed or the watchdog running out decides it; the watchdog decides it as nothing found (ADR 0097). A halt holds the look back and the team's tap resumes it. A look that failed (no network, a refused device) is decided as a failure: over an Opening it rests at the quiet invite, over a spoken turn it shows the person sign, and while the room is still answering it looks again. A reply that landed plays as if it had arrived on time, except an Opening found after the room said it was still answering, which rests silent with «Ouvir de novo» armed. Anything else raises a blocking Halt that calls for a person and keeps the take, except over an Opening, which rests at the quiet invite, and over a line said again, which rests with «Ouvir de novo» still armed.
_Avoid_: retry, resend, poll

**Session read**:
The room's reading of the session on the server, applied whole whichever door it came through: its halt, its warning, its stretches and its verdict.
_Avoid_: snapshot (the wire's name), poll, re-read

**Session gone**:
The server no longer knows the session. Wherever the room learns it, whatever the tablet held of the session in memory is let go and the team is taken to the Choice; its takes still unsent stay on the tablet for the passage's next session.
_Avoid_: 404, dead session, lost session

**Passage closed**:
The server's refusal of the call for a person because the passage is already finished (ADR 0052).
_Avoid_: finished session, PASSAGE_CLOSED (the wire's code)

**Refusal**:
The server's answer that it will not do what was asked, naming why with a code; the room tells refusals apart by the code, never by the words.
_Avoid_: error, failure, detail

**Meaning Map**:
The passage's canonical content, against which the analyst compares what was told back, including what is deliberately left unrevealed.
_Avoid_: mapa de sentido, answer key, base text

**Team-talk**:
The turn in which the server sends the team to talk among themselves: the room waits, records nothing, and shows it is waiting rather than listening.
_Avoid_: conversa entre a equipe, peer cue, rehearsal (the station), pause

**Claim code**:
The code the server mints for one tablet, which a facilitator spends once from the Desk to link that tablet to a team.
_Avoid_: código, pairing code, device code, credential (what the link yields afterwards)

**Device link**:
The bond between one tablet and one team, drawn once a claim code is spent, and carrying the device credential: the only thing the tablet sends to be let in, on every request thereafter. Unlinking the tablet from the Desk revokes the credential, and the tablet returns to a fresh claim code.
_Avoid_: vínculo, pairing, enrolment

**Life**:
The room from the moment a device link opens it until the link is revoked or the app ends. Everything the room keeps in memory is born with it, and nothing of one life reaches the next (ADR 0070); an answer from an earlier life is dropped by its generation.
_Avoid_: instance, launch (a launch begins a life; a relink begins another), session (a session is the server's)
